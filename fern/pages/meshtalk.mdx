# MeshTalk

Infrastructure-free walkie-talkie for RayNeo X3 Pro AR glasses. Voice-activated transmission via Silero VAD, Speex AEC for bone conduction echo cancellation, Opus codec for efficient audio, WiFi Aware for direct glasses-to-glasses communication — no server, phone, or internet needed.

See [DESIGN.md](../meshtalk/DESIGN.md) for the full specification.
